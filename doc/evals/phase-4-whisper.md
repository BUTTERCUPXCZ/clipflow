### Findings (2026-11-26, written by hand after the run)

Machine: the target laptop (Ryzen, 16 GB RAM, Whisper on CPU int8). The first run included the one-time 1.5 GB download of `medium` in STRUKTURA's time (348 s); the table below is the re-run with the model cached.

- **Speed:** medium takes about **1.9× as long** as small: 97 s vs 52 s for 11:20 of audio (0.14× vs 0.08× real time). For a 30-minute video that is about 4.3 min vs 2.3 min, which is still well below the ~7–10 min of frame analysis.
- **Quality**, on a 100 s stretch of fast game commentary with crowd noise (SEN TenZ_ special, 248–340 s):
  - Medium gets several phrases right that small garbles: "Attempting to **punish**" (small: "put this"); "I certainly think **so**" (small: "sorry"); "backstab through window and **blindside Dapper**" (small: "behind slide dapper"); "try and retake, **almost deny the plant**" (small: "to hide the plant"); "Oh my god".
  - Small **hallucinated** "Thank you for watching." at 424 s, a known Whisper failure on music or silence, and that text reached the transcript. Medium did not.
  - Both still miss names ("TenZ" → "tens") and some caster jargon. This is a hard input; neither is clean.
- **STRUKTURA** (app walkthrough, 1:30): **no speech detected** by either model. Its audio is music only, so clip planning correctly reports `NO_SPEECH` for it once it is processed.

**Recommendation:** switch the default to `TRANSCRIPTION_MODEL=medium`. It costs about 1 extra minute per 15 minutes of video on this laptop, and it gives fewer garbled phrases and no "thank you for watching" hallucination. Clip titles, hooks and captions are written from the transcript, so they improve directly. Keep `small` as an option for quick drafts.
